Protocol: RREV3; Hub and Agent must use compatible versions
Lanes: four independently authenticated TCP/TLS lanes by default; each reconnects independently with jittered exponential backoff

Cancellation: pending target dials are cancelled on CLOSE or lane shutdown; late successful dials are discarded safely
Close-race handling: expected EOF/net.ErrClosed/context cancellation and common closed-stream races are suppressed; actual open failures are rate-limited
Lane diagnostics: session endings are reported by reason category without exposing endpoint addresses
